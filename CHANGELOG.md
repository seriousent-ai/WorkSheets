# Changes

## Phase 9
- 8 more advanced entries (231 in all): euro step finishing, shooting off the dribble, pocket and hook passing, 3-on-2 and 2-on-1 decisions (Steve Nash), a 1-on-1 contain defense drill and a decision-making progression. One is a YouTube video; the rest are web pages that open in the browser.
- Watch non-stop: a new "All topics" chip plays a shuffled mix of every topic (up to 60 videos), for the car, the bus or the sofa.
- Curriculum (Guide): 29 fixed steps run on Tuesday and Friday: the six themed sessions for each of the four cycle weeks, a check-up after each, and a finish line. He cannot move to the next step until the current one is finished, however long that takes. A step that is not finished comes back on the next step day. Videos are encouraged but optional. Check-ups hold the skill ladder tests, and checking one off passes that rung. A parent can unlock or go back a step in Family view.
- Start from scratch (Guide, Curriculum): a backup is saved, training progress and the game log are cleared, the real team schedule (Rens practice, skills training, Wednesday league game, repeating every week) is added, and Monday becomes a rest day. The start day is Tuesday, November 3. Progress dated before the start is ignored, so it cannot return from the server or another phone.
- Team events can repeat every week.
- Library cleanup: videos for young children, beginners and Jr. NBA kids were removed. 223 entries remain (126 Intermediate, 97 Advanced), all aimed at ages 12 and up.
- Tutorials: every required curriculum block (warm-up, session blocks, ladder tests, film note, leadership goal) shows a "How to do it (videos)" row of 1 to 3 library videos in its details. They are optional.
- 27 more advanced entries (added before the cleanup): closeouts and help defense, defending bigger players, free throws under pressure, two-ball dribbling, off-ball movement, point guard film study and a 3-on-2 transition drill.
- Fix: on a phone with Family view on, the progress overview that opens at launch said the phone was not connected until Refresh was tapped. The app now loads the family code before the first screen is drawn, and the overview updates itself when the first sync finishes.
- Drill details have a "Make this harder" button with a harder version for each core drill, by day.
- "Watch, then try it" (Videos tab): seven advanced videos each paired with a short drill (+15 XP).
- Film notes (Videos tab): one thing seen and one thing to fix (+5 XP each).
- Game day card on Today with a pre-game checklist, and three after-game questions (good read, mistake, change) saved with the game.
- Leadership goal each week (Log tab, +15 XP), with another goal button.
- "This week in plain words" at the top of the parent progress overview, also added to the shared weekly report.
- Skill ladder (Log tab): five rungs for each of the five skills, each with a test such as "pull-up off one dribble from the elbow: 6 of 10". Tapping Passed it gives 30 XP and shows the next test. Synced between phones.
- Game stats: record, steals per game, and the last five games against the season, next to the assist to turnover number.
- Shot chart: a glow behind each spot, how each spot compares with the four weeks before, and a line naming the most improved and weakest spot.
- Video library: 229 suggested videos on offense, defense, teamwork, point guard play and warm-up. Each is tagged Basics, Intermediate or Advanced; only Advanced shows by default (a level switch brings the rest back).
- "Watch non-stop" plays a whole topic as one YouTube playlist, with optional shuffle. Paste a YouTube video or playlist link to add it. Add to my videos, or hide, any suggestion.
- 67 more advanced entries added over four rounds (296 in all): workouts, pick-and-roll defense, pull-ups and step-backs, floaters, passing, defensive footwork, speed and agility, small-sided games, plus 22 written guides that open in the browser.
- Video notes no longer repeat the source name. The whole library is written down in `docs/VIDEO-LIBRARY.md`, with the ladder tests and drill versions, and `docs/PROJECT-NOTES.md` has a Phase 9 section.
- Titles and links come from web searches and were not all watched; a parent should check them.
