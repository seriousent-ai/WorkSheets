# Video library and Track 7 content

This file records the suggested videos and the written content added in Phase 9. The source of truth is `data.js` (`SUGGESTED_VIDEOS`, `LADDER`, `LEVELUP`, `DECISIONS`, `LEAD_PROMPTS`, `PREGAME`). This list was generated from it.

**How these were chosen:** titles and links came from web searches. They were not watched, and the build machine could not open YouTube. A parent should skim the list, and use Hide in the app for anything that does not fit. Some videos may refuse to play inside the app; those show an Open on YouTube link. Entries that are not on YouTube (Breakthrough Basketball, Jr. NBA, LevelUp Basketball and others) open in the browser and cannot play non-stop.

**Levels:** 2 Intermediate, 3 Advanced (no Basics). The app shows only Advanced by default (Level switch under Watch non-stop). Levels were set from titles and my judgement, not from watching.

Totals: 274 entries (Intermediate 146, Advanced 128); 228 play in the app, 46 open in the browser.

## Skill ladder tests

Five rungs per skill. Passing a rung gives 30 XP.

### Ball handling

1. Two-ball stationary dribble for 30 seconds with no lost ball
2. Cone course with both hands in under 12 seconds, no lost ball
3. Change of pace into a crossover against a live defender: get past 5 of 5 times
4. Weak-hand-only dribble up the floor against pressure: no turnovers in 3 trips
5. Escape a trap with a pivot or a split against two defenders: 4 of 5 reps

### Passing and reads

1. 20 chest and bounce passes on target in one minute with a partner (18 or more)
2. Left-hand pocket passes: 8 of 10 on target
3. Pass off the dribble on the move: 8 of 10 hit the target hand
4. Ball-screen read, 3 on 2: the right pass 4 of 5 times
5. Skip pass across the floor: 8 of 10 on target and in rhythm

### Shooting

1. Form shooting from 5 feet: 8 of 10
2. Free throws: 8 of 10 in two different weeks
3. Catch and shoot: 7 of 10 from his best spot
4. Pull-up off one dribble from the elbow: 6 of 10
5. Off the dribble at game speed from three spots: 5 of 10 from each

### Finishing and defense

1. Layups with both hands: 8 of 10 on each side
2. Euro-step or reverse layup against a pad or defender: 6 of 10
3. Finish through contact against a pad: 6 of 10
4. Floater over a defender or a chair: 5 of 10
5. Contain a dribbler for 10 seconds without a foul, 3 times in a row

### Court IQ

1. Name the defense (man, zone or press) at the start of 3 possessions in a game
2. Watch a clip and write down 3 reads before the play ends
3. Explain drop, hedge and switch on a ball screen, and your best attack against each
4. Hold an assist to turnover ratio of 2.5 or better over a game
5. Lead a timeout: tell the team one adjustment and why

## Watch, then try it

- **Read the hard hedge** (video btpuIhLSg8E): Set two chairs as a screen with a partner as the big. Dribble at it and call the coverage out loud: hedge. Then reject or split. 5 reps each way.
- **Attack drop coverage** (video TbovZAZRRL4): Have a partner back up under the screen. Pull up if he drops. Take 8 shots, then attack the gap and finish 4 times.
- **Study a great point guard** (video qUqvYc773qY): Watch it twice. The second time, pause 3 times before the pass and say what the guard sees. Write down one read to copy.
- **Change of pace** (video K5f-E_-if00): Walk, then explode, for 5 trips each side. Go from half speed to full speed in two steps, with your eyes up.
- **The pocket pass** (video lt3BlXJx3do): Dribble toward a chair, then pass through a gap to a partner. 10 passes with each hand. The pass should be low and on target.
- **Contain a quick guard** (video sEn1sQkmJkI): Slide and keep your chest in front of a partner. Stop him from reaching the free-throw line for 10 seconds. Do 3 in a row.
- **Make quicker decisions** (video j16FCb3BbDs): Watch each clip and pause. Say the decision out loud before the answer. Then repeat the best one in a 2 on 1.

## Harder drill versions (Make this harder)

### handle

- Monday: Do the whole block with a defender in a chair-and-cone gauntlet: attack, change pace, and keep your eyes up. Add a second ball for the last 3 minutes.
- Tuesday: Add a live defender who only plays the first two dribbles. Try a hesitation and a snatchback before the crossover.
- Wednesday: Weak-hand-only for the full block, then a 30-second go at game speed against a partner who reaches.
- Thursday: Have a partner pressure full-court. Beat the trap with a pivot, a split or a retreat dribble, and count turnovers. Aim for none in 3 trips.
- Friday: Push the ball up the floor at full speed, and decide at the line: pull up, hit a trailer, or attack. Do 10 trips and keep a score.
- Saturday: Run the cone course against a clock and add a counter move after every crossover. Beat last week's time.

### pass

- Monday: Pass to a moving target and add a defender with one hand up. Count turnovers and keep them under 2.
- Tuesday: Run 3 on 2 and make the read in under 2 seconds. Hit the open man with a one-hand or skip pass.
- Wednesday: Add a pass fake, then a pocket pass or a wrap pass. Aim for 8 of 10 on target.
- Thursday: Throw passes against a live defender who is trying to steal. Add a ball-screen read: reject, split or pocket pass to the roller.
- Friday: Live 2 on 1 and 3 on 2 on the break. Pass ahead early and aim a lead pass at the receiver's far hand.
- Saturday: Play 3 on 3 with two dribbles maximum. Count assists and turnovers and write the ratio down.

### shoot

- Monday: Shoot from the elbow off one dribble. Move for every catch. Aim for 6 of 10, then step back one foot.
- Tuesday: Use a pull-up off a ball screen from a chair. Read the defender: pull up if he drops, attack if he steps up.
- Wednesday: Shoot off a step-back or snatchback. Keep the form the same as the close-range work. 5 of 10.
- Thursday: Add a closeout defender with a hand up. Catch, one dribble, shoot over the hand. Count makes in a row.
- Friday: Catch on the run in transition and shoot in rhythm. 10 shots from three different spots at game speed.
- Saturday: 25 shots from five spots, but each one after a move: a dribble pull-up, a hesitation or a step-back. Compare with last week.

### finish

- Monday: Finish with a Euro-step or a reverse layup against a pad. 6 of 10 on each side.
- Tuesday: On defense, mirror a partner who changes pace, then close out and contain for 10 seconds without fouling.
- Wednesday: Finish through contact: take a bump from a pad and still make the layup. Add a floater over a chair.
- Thursday: Defend a live dribbler for 10 seconds without a foul. Keep your chest in front and your hands up. Do 3 in a row.
- Friday: Finish at speed after a controlled approach, with a defender trailing. Use either hand and a floater when the big steps up.
- Saturday: Play 1 on 1 to 5 points. Win with a finish through contact and a stop on defense.
